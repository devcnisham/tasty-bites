# Five-minute studio tour

1. **Welcome (45 seconds).** In a fresh tab, open `/`. Watch the large plate-and-cutlery symbol and the small falling macarons. The intro runs once per tab. Show blush colors, bow details and floating bakes. Open cookie Customise; optional switches start off.
2. **Find a bake (45 seconds).** Browse courses, select Macarons, and open Macarons Made Easy. Expand the syllabus and explore its free-preview lesson.
3. **Practice purchase (60 seconds).** Add Macarons to the bag and apply `WELCOME10`. Show GST and the discount. Continue with made-up checkout details, open mock UPI payment and use `demo@upi`. Optionally enable the failed-payment demonstration, then retry. Show the receipt and unlocked course.
4. **Your kitchen (60 seconds).** Open My learning. Play, pause, seek and change speed. Scale recipe servings, tick ingredients and save a note. With Preferences enabled, play past ten seconds and revisit to show resume. Mark a lesson complete and inspect dashboard progress.
5. **Behind the counter (60 seconds).** Switch to demo admin. Show charts, edit a course, add lessons, reorder phases, save, simulate a video upload and create a coupon. Custom coupons demonstrate management; checkout uses the two built-in codes.
6. **Finishing details (30 seconds).** Reopen Cookie settings, change optional categories and toggle Alt+D to see sent/blocked events. Show dark mode. Return home: the intro does not replay.

Accounts, business figures, quotes, payments, ingredients and uploads are illustrative. No money, external tracking or messages are sent. Real photos, verified studio content and the production backend come later.

Show the scroll-eaten cookie, generated Moon Cake class poster and offer carousel. Reel previews stay still; click to open a portrait film. The separate landscape film is on About. Admin → Reels keeps the saved layout in this browser.
